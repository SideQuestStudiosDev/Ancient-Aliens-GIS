Ancient Aliens GIS

What this is

A 3D geoportal for every location referenced in the television series Ancient Aliens. Each site is presented in three parts — the Claim made on screen, the Record as described by Wikipedia and archaeological sources, and Context noting where the two diverge.

This is a reference and mapping tool, not an endorsement. The Claim section reports what the series asserts. The Record and Context sections report the mainstream scholarly position. Where a claim has no evidentiary support, the dossier says so.

Navigating

  • Drag to orbit · scroll / pinch to zoom · right-drag / two-finger drag to tilt.
  • Pick any site from the list or search to fly there.
  • Click a marker on the globe to open its dossier.

Keyboard

  • / focus search · Esc close
  • 123 3D / 2.5D / 2D projection
  • H reset view · L my location · F toggle FX
  • Tab-navigable throughout; ↑↓ move through search results

Standards & sources

Sites are served as OGC API — Features (GeoJSON, EPSG:4326 / CRS84). Imagery arrives over OGC WMTS and XYZ tile services; terrain is a quantised-mesh elevation service. See the OGC tab for live endpoints and declared conformance classes.

Descriptive text in the Record section is drawn from Wikipedia and is reused under CC BY-SA 4.0. Imagery and basemap attributions appear at the lower-right of the globe.
